## Who we are
We are a group of voluntary developers, designers and engineers developing [Luminate OS](https://luminateos.xyz). Our mission is to make a beautiful, yet, simple and lightweight smartphone operating system and Launcher and also Apps. 

## How to apply
We are open to new developers who can contribute to Luminate OS. If you wish to apply, you can make a ticket and apply on our [Discord](http://dsc.gg/luminate). Everyone who can contribute to our team are welcome!
